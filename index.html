PuzzleMob Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

PuzzleMob respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and processed when you use the PuzzleMob mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

PuzzleMob does not require users to provide personal information such as a name, home address or phone number in order to play.

Certain information may be collected automatically by third-party services used by the application. This may include device information, advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information and other technical data.

2. Advertising

PuzzleMob uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner ads and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address and interactions with advertisements in accordance with their privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

PuzzleMob uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy and advertising consent where required by applicable laws.

Where required, users may be presented with options regarding advertising and the processing of their information.

4. Analytics

PuzzleMob uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand how the application is used and to help improve the game.

Analytics information may include application interactions, device information, general usage information and technical information.

Firebase Privacy and Security

5. User Photos

PuzzleMob allows users to select photos from their device and turn them into jigsaw puzzles.

Photos selected by the user are used to provide the requested puzzle functionality. PuzzleMob does not sell users' photos or use them for advertising purposes.

Photo access may be managed through Android's system photo picker or device permissions.

6. Game Progress and Local Data

PuzzleMob may store information related to puzzle progress, completed puzzles, favorites, rewards, diamonds, settings and gameplay preferences.

This information may be stored locally on the user's device to provide game functionality and preserve progress.

7. Notifications

PuzzleMob may send game-related notifications, including reminders about daily puzzles and rewards.

Users can disable notifications at any time through their Android device settings.

8. Third-Party Services

PuzzleMob uses services provided by Google, including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Play Services

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

PuzzleMob does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so appropriate action can be taken.

10. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with the use of PuzzleMob. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information and advertising preferences.

Users can also manage application permissions and notification settings through their Android device.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to PuzzleMob, third-party services or applicable requirements.

Changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be updated accordingly.
